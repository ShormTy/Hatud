Privacy notice

Last updated: November 4, 2026. Hatud is a small pilot run by one person. This notice is written in plain language and is a draft for the owner to have reviewed (Data Privacy Act of 2012, RA 10173).

Who is responsible

The Hatud owner is responsible for the information in this app. Write to hatud.support@gmail.com for any question about it.

What Hatud keeps

  • Everyone: your email address and password (the password is kept by Google Firebase in a scrambled form; the owner cannot read it), your name, your contact number, whether you are a student or a driver, the date you signed up, and the date you agreed to this notice.
  • Students: your school.
  • Drivers: your vehicle type and plate number, and whether the owner has approved you.
  • Rides: the pickup and drop-off points and their names, the fare, the status of the ride, and the time it was made. Ride records are kept for about 30 days and are then deleted when you next open the app.
  • Reports: if you report the other person on a ride, or the app, the reason and the optional note you write. For a report about a person, Hatud also keeps a private list of the different people who reported that person (only the owner can see it).
  • Blocks and appeals: whether your account is blocked, whether the owner or the automatic rule blocked it, and the message of any appeal you send.

A driver’s phone uses its own location to show distances and routes. Hatud does not save it. A student’s “Use my location” fills in the pickup point of that one ride.

Who can see it

  • The owner can read all of the above, to approve drivers, read reports and appeals, and block or unblock an account. A person who is reported is never told who reported him.
  • A driver’s name, vehicle and contact number are shown to a student only after that driver accepts the student’s ride. A student’s name and contact number are shown to the driver who accepts the ride.
  • Approved drivers can see the pickup and drop-off points of open requests.
  • Nothing is sold, and there are no ads.

Outside services Hatud uses

  • Google Firebase keeps the accounts and the data and hosts this site. The data is stored on servers in Singapore. Firebase also sends the password reset email.
  • OpenStreetMap provides the map pictures, and its free routing service (OSRM) draws road routes. To do this, the pickup, drop-off and driver’s points are sent to them.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs) delivers the map software (Leaflet) to your phone.
  • Google Forms is used only if you choose to send feedback.

Reports, blocking and appeals

After a ride, a student or a driver can report the other person, or the app. If 3 different people report the same person, that person’s account is blocked automatically. A blocked person cannot request or accept new rides (a ride already in progress can still be finished), but can send one appeal at a time to the owner. The owner reads appeals and reports, and can unblock the person; unblocking clears the list of people who reported him and closes the appeal. Reports and appeals are deleted by the owner when they are no longer needed.

Money

Hatud does not handle payments. Fares are agreed between student and driver and paid in cash.

Your choices

You can ask the owner to see, correct or delete your account and your data at any time by writing to hatud.support@gmail.com from the email you signed up with. You can also delete your own account inside the app (“Delete my account” at the bottom of your page). It is deleted 24 hours after you ask, so the other person on a recent ride has time to report a problem; you can undo it until then. After the 24 hours you type your password, and it deletes your profile, your vehicle details (drivers), your finished rides (students) and your login. A blocked account can’t be deleted that way (write to the owner), and a block that arrives during the 24 hours stops the deletion. Some records stay for a while: a ride you gave as a driver stays in the student’s history until its 30 days are over, and reports about you or sent by you stay with the owner until the owner deletes them.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes too.
