Rides for students

Need a ride to school? Ask, agree on the fare, go.

Hatud connects students with approved local drivers. Pin where you are and where you are going, a driver nearby accepts, and you agree on the fare before the ride starts.

How it works

For students

  1. Pin your pickup and drop-off on the map and send your request.
  2. A driver accepts and names a fare. Agree to it, send one lower offer, or decline and let another driver take it.
  3. Meet your driver and pay in cash.

For drivers

  1. Sign up. The Hatud owner meets you and approves your vehicle in person.
  2. See open requests near you, nearest first, and accept the ones you want at your own fare.
  3. Tap On the way and Completed as the ride goes.

Made with safety in mind

  • Drivers are approved in person before they can see any request.
  • Your name and phone number are shared only with the driver who accepts your ride, and only after you agree to his fare. The driver’s details are shown to you.
  • Hatud does not follow your location. You choose the pickup and drop-off pins.
  • Something went wrong? Either side can report the other person or the app, and the owner can block an account.
  • No payments in the app. Fares are agreed first and paid in cash.

A pilot for students

Hatud is a small pilot. To sign up as a student you need the invite code from the Hatud owner, usually shared in your class group chat. Drivers sign up and then wait for approval.
